# Content Frameworks

Reference guide for content pillars, hook patterns, content types, and topic generation. Load on demand during Strategy, Create, and Calendar modes.

---

## 1. Content Pillar Framework

Every piece of content must fit into one of 3-5 pillars. Pillars are thematic buckets tied to your brand and goals.

### Default Pillar Templates (Adapt for Specific Brand)

| Pillar | Description | Content Types | Weight |
|--------|-------------|--------------|--------|
| **Product Building** | Your journey building the product — decisions, trade-offs, lessons | product_update, personal_narrative, case_study | 25-35% |
| **Industry Insights** | Opinions on trends, tools, and developments in your space | opinion, industry_commentary, curation | 20-30% |
| **Educational** | Teaching your audience something useful related to your domain | educational, case_study | 15-25% |
| **Founder Journey** | Personal experiences as a solo founder — struggles, wins, reflections | personal_narrative, opinion | 15-20% |
| **Customer/User Stories** | Results, feedback, use cases from users (or hypothetical scenarios pre-launch) | case_study, product_update | 10-15% |

### Pillar Weight by Product Stage

| Stage | Primary Pillar | Secondary | Reduce |
|-------|---------------|-----------|--------|
| **Pre-launch / Idea** | Industry Insights (40%) | Founder Journey (30%) | Customer Stories (0%) |
| **Building / Pre-demo** | Product Building (35%) | Industry Insights (25%) | Customer Stories (5%) |
| **Has Demo / Beta** | Product Building (30%) | Educational (25%) | — |
| **Has Customers** | Customer Stories (30%) | Educational (25%) | Founder Journey (10%) |
| **Growth** | Educational (30%) | Customer Stories (25%) | Founder Journey (10%) |

## 2. Content Type Definitions

| Type | Description | When to Use | Density |
|------|-------------|-------------|---------|
| **opinion** | Your take on something. Contrarian, specific, experience-based. | Always. Core of personal brand. | Medium |
| **case_study** | Breakdown of a specific situation with details and lessons. | When you have real data or experience. | Heavy |
| **product_update** | Feature launch, milestone, behind-the-scenes of building. | When shipping. Max 1-2x/week. | Light-Medium |
| **industry_commentary** | React to news, launches, trends in your space. | When something relevant happens. | Medium |
| **personal_narrative** | Founder story, lesson learned, vulnerable moment. | 1-2x/week max. High engagement. | Medium |
| **educational** | Teach a concept, framework, or skill. | When audience needs education on your domain. | Heavy |
| **curation** | Share and comment on others' content, tools, resources. | Fill gaps in calendar. Low effort, moderate value. | Light |

### Content Density Levels

| Level | Description | Platform Fit |
|-------|-------------|-------------|
| **Heavy** | Deep technical content, data-rich, requires thinking time | Newsletter, Blog, LinkedIn long-form |
| **Medium** | Structured insight, clear takeaway, some depth | LinkedIn, X thread, Threads |
| **Light** | Quick take, observation, reaction, one insight | X single post, Threads, quick LinkedIn |

## 3. Hook Patterns

The first line determines whether anyone reads the rest. These patterns are proven scroll-stoppers.

### Pattern 1: Counterintuitive Statement
> "The best marketing strategy for a solo founder is to stop marketing."

### Pattern 2: Specific Number / Result
> "I grew from 0 to 500 users in 30 days. Here's exactly what I did."

### Pattern 3: Contrarian Take
> "Most startup advice is written for VC-funded companies. Ignore it."

### Pattern 4: Vulnerability / Confession
> "I almost quit last month. Here's what changed my mind."

### Pattern 5: Question That Hits a Nerve
> "Why do we keep building features nobody asked for?"

### Pattern 6: "Nobody Talks About This"
> "The part of solo founding nobody warns you about:"

### Pattern 7: Before/After or Then/Now
> "6 months ago I couldn't get a single customer. Yesterday I hit $5K MRR."

### Pattern 8: Pattern Interrupt
> "Stop. Before you write another line of code, read this."

### Pattern 9: Hot Take with Specificity
> "Notion is killing solo founders' productivity. Here's why I switched to plain text."

### Pattern 10: Direct Address
> "If you're a developer trying to build a SaaS solo, this is for you."

### Hook Anti-Patterns (Never Use)
- "Just a quick thought..." (screams low value)
- "I'm excited to announce..." (corporate, boring)
- "In today's fast-paced world..." (generic, AI-smell)
- "Thread 🧵" as the entire hook (lazy)
- Starting with a link or @mention (killed by algorithm)
- "This might be unpopular but..." (overused, try-hard)

## 4. CTA Patterns

Every post needs a CTA. But not every CTA is "buy my product."

### Engagement CTAs (Most Posts)
- "What's your experience with [X]? Drop it below."
- "Agree or disagree?"
- "What would you add to this list?"
- "Tag someone who needs to hear this."

### Growth CTAs (1-2x/week)
- "Follow me for more [topic] content."
- "If this resonated, repost so others see it."
- "I write about [topic] every [day]. Follow along."

### Conversion CTAs (Max 1-2x/week)
- "I'm building [product] to solve exactly this. Link in bio."
- "If you want [outcome], try [product] — link in reply."
- "I wrote a detailed guide on this. Subscribe to my newsletter: [link]"

### CTA Rules
1. **Match CTA to post type**: Opinion posts → engagement CTA. Product posts → conversion CTA.
2. **Never use conversion CTA on every post** — max 20% of posts.
3. **Soft CTA > Hard CTA**: "I write about this weekly" > "Buy my product now"
4. **Question CTAs** drive the most comments (algorithmic boost).

## 5. Topic Generation System

### Source 1: Audience Questions
- Monitor comments, DMs, and replies for recurring questions
- Each question = at least 1 post (answer + expand)
- High-frequency questions → content series

### Source 2: Personal Experience
- What did you learn this week?
- What mistake did you make?
- What decision are you wrestling with?
- What surprised you about your data?

### Source 3: Industry Events
- Product launches by competitors or adjacent tools
- Funding announcements in your space
- Industry reports or data releases
- Policy changes affecting your market
- AI model releases, platform changes

### Source 4: Product Milestones
- Feature shipped → "behind the scenes" post
- Metric hit → "here's how" post
- Bug found → "what I learned" post
- Customer feedback → "what users taught me" post

### Source 5: Recycling High Performers
- Take your best-performing post from 30+ days ago
- Rewrite with a new angle, updated data, or different format
- Cross-post to a platform it hasn't been on

### Topic Freshness Rules
- **Same topic**: Can repeat every 4-6 weeks with a new angle
- **Same angle**: Must wait 8+ weeks before reusing
- **Same format**: Vary format even when repeating topic (post → thread → carousel)

## 6. Content Series Templates

Recurring series build audience habits and reduce planning overhead.

| Series Type | Example | Frequency | Platform |
|-------------|---------|-----------|----------|
| **Weekly Build Log** | "This week I built: [features]. This week I learned: [lessons]." | Weekly | X, LinkedIn |
| **Founder Lessons** | "Lesson #N from building [product] solo:" | 2-3x/week | X |
| **Tool Reviews** | "I tried [tool] for a week. Here's my honest take." | Biweekly | LinkedIn, Newsletter |
| **Metric Updates** | "Month N: [users], [revenue], [key metric]. Here's what changed:" | Monthly | X thread, LinkedIn |
| **Ask Me Anything** | "Building [product]. AMA about [topic]." | Monthly | X, Threads |
| **Industry Reactions** | Quick take on breaking news in your space | As-needed | X |

## 7. Avoiding Repetition

### The Angle Rotation System

For any given topic, rotate through these angles:

1. **The What**: What happened / what is it
2. **The Why**: Why it matters / why I did it
3. **The How**: How it works / how I built it
4. **The Lesson**: What I learned from it
5. **The Mistake**: What went wrong
6. **The Comparison**: How it compares to alternatives
7. **The Prediction**: What this means for the future
8. **The Question**: Asking the audience their take

**Rule**: Never post the same topic with the same angle within 6 weeks.

### Staleness Signals
- You've used the same hook pattern 3x in a row
- Your last 5 posts are all the same content type
- You haven't posted from 2+ pillars in the past week
- Your engagement is dropping (audience fatigue)
