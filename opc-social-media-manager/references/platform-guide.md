# Platform-Specific Guide

Reference guide for platform rules, formats, and optimization. Load on demand during Create and Repurpose modes.

---

## 1. X (Twitter)

### Goals
- Awareness and reach
- Thought leadership / hot takes
- Virality through opinions and frameworks

### Format Rules

| Constraint | Rule |
|-----------|------|
| **Single post** | Max 280 chars (standard) / 4,000 chars (X Premium). Aim for 180-240 chars for max engagement. |
| **Thread** | 3-12 tweets. First tweet is the hook — must stand alone. Last tweet has CTA. Number tweets (1/n) or use narrative flow. |
| **Hook** | Must stop the scroll in first 7 words. No "I think..." or "Just a thought..." openers. |
| **Line breaks** | Use blank lines between ideas. One idea per paragraph. |
| **Hashtags** | 0-2 max. In-body, not appended. Never more than 2. |
| **Emoji** | Sparingly — 0-2 per post. Never as bullet points unless brand voice calls for it. |
| **Links** | Reduce engagement. Put in reply, not main post. Exception: launches and announcements. |
| **Images** | Increase engagement 2-3x. Use screenshots, charts, diagrams. |
| **Tagging** | Tag people only when genuinely relevant. Never mass-tag. |

### Tone
- Direct, opinionated, concise
- Hot takes welcome — nuanced takes get less reach
- "Building in public" voice works well
- Contrarian > consensus
- Personal experience > generic advice

### What Performs Well
- Counterintuitive observations ("The thing nobody tells you about [X]...")
- Frameworks and lists ("5 things I learned from...")
- Build logs and metrics (real numbers)
- Relatable founder struggles
- One strong opinion per post

### What Performs Poorly
- Corporate speak
- Vague motivational content
- Links in main post body
- Long paragraphs without line breaks
- Threads without a strong first tweet

## 2. LinkedIn

### Goals
- Professional authority
- Lead generation
- Industry credibility
- Hiring/partnership visibility

### Format Rules

| Constraint | Rule |
|-----------|------|
| **Post length** | 1,200-1,500 chars optimal. Max 3,000 chars. |
| **Hook** | First 2 lines visible before "see more" — must compel click. |
| **Structure** | Use line breaks aggressively. Short paragraphs (1-3 sentences). White space matters. |
| **Hashtags** | 3-5, placed at end. Mix niche + broad. |
| **Emoji** | More acceptable than X. Use for bullet points and section markers. |
| **Links** | Put in comments, not post body. LinkedIn suppresses link posts. |
| **Carousel** | PDF upload as carousel. 8-12 slides. Works extremely well. |
| **Tagging** | Tag companies and people for amplification. Max 3-5 tags. |

### Tone
- Professional but not stiff
- Storytelling format works best
- First person, vulnerable, specific
- "I was wrong about [X]" outperforms "Here's how to [X]"
- Data and specifics > vague claims

### Structure Template
```
[Hook line — provocative or surprising]

[Empty line]

[Context — 2-3 sentences of backstory]

[Empty line]

[Core insight — the main point, 3-5 sentences]

[Empty line]

[Takeaway — what the reader should do differently]

[Empty line]

[CTA — question to drive comments, or ask to follow/share]

[Empty line]

#hashtag1 #hashtag2 #hashtag3
```

### What Performs Well
- Personal failure → lesson learned stories
- "I spent $X on [thing], here's what I learned"
- Contrarian industry takes with data
- Hiring/culture posts
- Celebrating milestones with backstory

### What Performs Poorly
- Resharing articles with no commentary
- Pure promotional posts
- Formal/corporate tone
- No personal stake or vulnerability
- Wall of text without line breaks

## 3. Threads

### Goals
- Community building
- Casual audience expansion
- Cross-posting from X with adaptation

### Format Rules

| Constraint | Rule |
|-----------|------|
| **Post length** | Max 500 chars. Aim for 200-400. |
| **Images** | Very effective. Carousels up to 10 images. |
| **Links** | Allowed in posts. |
| **Hashtags** | Not functional yet (as of early 2025). Avoid. |
| **Tone** | More casual than X. Conversational. |

### Adaptation from X
- Expand slightly — more context allowed
- More conversational tone
- Images even more important
- Less "hot take" culture, more community

## 4. Bluesky

### Goals
- Early adopter community
- Tech-savvy audience
- Alternative to X for developer audience

### Format Rules

| Constraint | Rule |
|-----------|------|
| **Post length** | Max 300 chars. |
| **Links** | Embeds work well. Links encouraged. |
| **Hashtags** | Not really used. |
| **Tone** | Nerdy, technical, less performative than X. |

### Notes
- Smaller audience but high engagement
- Developer/tech audience overrepresented
- Less algorithmic — chronological feed matters
- Custom feeds are a unique distribution mechanism

## 5. Newsletter / Blog

### Goals
- SEO and long-term discoverability
- Deep authority on topics
- Email list building
- Repurposing source material

### Format Rules

| Constraint | Rule |
|-----------|------|
| **Length** | Newsletter: 500-1,500 words. Blog: 1,000-3,000 words. |
| **Structure** | Clear sections with headers. TL;DR at top. |
| **CTA** | Subscribe, share, or reply. Always present. |
| **Frequency** | Weekly or biweekly. Consistency > frequency. |

### Repurposing
- Blog post → 3-5 social posts
- Thread → newsletter section
- Social post that performed well → expanded blog post
- Newsletter → social excerpts for the week

## 6. Cross-Platform Repurposing Matrix

Same topic, different format per platform:

| Source | → X | → LinkedIn | → Threads | → Newsletter |
|--------|-----|-----------|-----------|-------------|
| **Opinion** | One strong take, <240 chars | Story format + takeaway + question | Conversational version | Deep dive with nuance |
| **Case study** | Key metric + one lesson | Full narrative arc + data | Quick visual summary | Detailed breakdown |
| **Product update** | Screenshot + one-liner | "Behind the scenes of building [X]" | Casual "shipped this today" | Feature spotlight with context |
| **Industry comment** | Hot take on the news | Structured analysis | Casual reaction | Thorough analysis |
| **Personal narrative** | Compressed lesson | Full story + vulnerability | Casual tone version | Reflective essay |

### Repurposing Rules
1. **Never copy-paste** across platforms. Each version must feel native.
2. **Start with the longest version** (newsletter/blog), then compress for shorter platforms.
3. **Or start with the core insight** and expand/compress in each direction.
4. **Change the hook** for each platform — what stops the scroll is different per platform.
5. **Change the CTA** — follow on X, connect on LinkedIn, subscribe for newsletter.

## 7. Posting Timing Guide

General best times (adjust based on audience):

| Platform | Best Days | Best Times (UTC) | Frequency |
|---------|-----------|-------------------|-----------|
| **X** | Tue-Thu | 13:00-16:00 | 1-3x/day |
| **LinkedIn** | Tue-Thu | 07:00-09:00, 12:00-14:00 | 3-5x/week |
| **Threads** | Mon-Fri | 11:00-15:00 | 3-5x/week |
| **Newsletter** | Tue or Thu | Morning local time | 1x/week |

### Rules
- Consistency > optimization. Pick a schedule and stick to it.
- Don't post on all platforms at the same time — stagger by 2-4 hours.
- Engage in comments for 30 minutes after posting — algorithmic boost.
